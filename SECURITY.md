# Security

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory on this repository, or contact the
maintainer directly. Please do not open a public issue.

## Third-party content in the sample corpus

`data/resumes/thomasdavis.json` is the public resume of Thomas Davis, founder
of [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org), included with his express
permission as both owner and subject. It is published by him at that site and
is reproduced here unaltered.

Every other document in `data/resumes/` is synthetic. Any resemblance to a
real person is coincidental.
